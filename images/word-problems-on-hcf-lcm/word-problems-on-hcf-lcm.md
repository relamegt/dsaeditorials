## Core Concepts and Fundamental Patterns

The **Highest Common Factor (HCF)**, also known as Greatest Common Divisor (GCD), of two or more numbers is the greatest number that divides each of them exactly without leaving any remainder.

### Primary Rules

- **Base Property**: Initial variables map directly to their standard mathematical forms.
- **Transformation Rule**: Operations must follow order limits to prevent calculation errors.

### How to Calculate / Find - Step-by-Step Methodology

To solve problems relating to this topic, follow these structured guidelines:

- **Standard Variation 1: Direct HCF**: Finding the highest common factor. Prime factorization: $HCF(a, b) = \text{product of lowest powers of common primes}$.
- **Standard Variation 2: HCF with different remainders**: Find the greatest number dividing $A, B, C$ leaving remainders $x, y, z \Rightarrow$ Find $\text{HCF}(A-x, B-y, C-z)$.
- **Standard Variation 3: HCF with same remainder (unknown)**: Find the greatest number dividing $A, B, C$ leaving the same remainder in each case $\Rightarrow$ Find $\text{HCF}(|A-B|, |B-C|, |C-A|)$.
- **Standard Variation 4: Minimum rows/boxes packing**: When packing items of sizes $A, B, C$ in equal racks without mixing. First find HCF ($H$), then total racks required $= \frac{A}{H} + \frac{B}{H} + \frac{C}{H}$.
- **For Fractions**: $\text{HCF of Fractions} = \frac{\text{HCF of Numerators}}{\text{LCM of Denominators}}$.

### Practical Calculations

- **Baker Has Chocolate Muffins**: A baker has 36 chocolate muffins and 48 blueberry muffins. He wants to arrange them in identical gift boxes such that each box contains the same number of chocolate muffins and the same number of blueberry muffins without any leftovers. What is the maximum number of identical gift boxes he can pack?
- *Step-by-Step*: The maximum number of identical boxes is given by the H.C.F. of 36 and 48, which is 12. (Result: **12**)
- **Three Alarm Clocks Office**: Three alarm clocks in an office ring at intervals of 10, 15, and 20 minutes respectively. If they all rang together at 9:00 AM, after how many minutes will they next ring together?
- *Step-by-Step*: The time when they ring together again is the L.C.M. of 10, 15, and 20, which is 60 minutes. (Result: **60 minutes**)

- *Step-by-Step*: The number of identical packets is the H.C.F. of 45 and 75, which is 15. Each packet will contain 45 / 15 = 3 pens. (Result: **3**)
- **Two Runner Athletes Are**: Two runner athletes are running on a circular track. Runner A completes one lap in 24 seconds, and Runner B completes one lap in 36 seconds. If they start running from the same point at the same time in the same direction, after how many seconds will they meet again at the starting point?
- *Step-by-Step*: The time they meet at the starting point is the L.C.M. of 24 and 36, which is 72 seconds. (Result: **72 seconds**)
