### Practical Calculations

- **Artisan Makes Custom Ceramic**: An artisan makes custom ceramic coffee mugs and packs them in boxes of 12. If she has 1,458 mugs in total, how many fully packed boxes will she have, and how many mugs will be left over?
- *Step-by-Step*: Divide 1,458 by 12. 1458 / 12 = 121 with a remainder of 6 (since 121 * 12 = 1452). (Result: **121 boxes, 6 mugs left**)
- **Digital Billboard Flashes Promotional**: A digital billboard flashes a promotional code every 15 seconds and an emergency alert every 24 seconds. If both flash together right now, after how many seconds will they flash together again?
- *Step-by-Step*: The time when both flash together again is the LCM of 15 and 24. LCM(15, 24) = 120 seconds. (Result: **120 seconds**)
- **Inventory Clerk Is Grouping**: An inventory clerk is grouping 36 blue pens, 48 black pens, and 60 red pens into identical gift sets with no pens left over. What is the maximum number of identical gift sets that can be created?
- *Step-by-Step*: The maximum number of identical sets is the HCF of 36, 48, and 60. HCF(36, 48, 60) = 12. (Result: **12 sets**)
- **Tech Companys Server Room**: A tech company's server room recorded an initial temperature of -4°C at midnight. By noon, the temperature rose by 17°C. What was the temperature at noon?
- *Step-by-Step*: Starting at -4°C and adding a rise of 17°C gives -4 + 17 = 13°C. (Result: **13°C**)

### Practical Calculations

- **Stock Clearance Sale Retail**: During a stock clearance sale, a retail store prices a jacket such that when it is divided into bundles of 5 items, 3 items remain, and when divided into bundles of 7 items, 5 items remain. What is the minimum possible stock count of these jackets?
- *Step-by-Step*: The remainders have a common difference of 2 (5-3 = 2, 7-5 = 2). The number is LCM(5, 7) - 2 = 35 - 2 = 33. (Result: **33**)
- **Quality Assurance Engineer Is**: A quality assurance engineer is testing a continuous stream of barcode scans. Every 6th scan triggers a system warning, and every 8th scan triggers a database backup. Which scan number will be the first one to trigger both events simultaneously?
- *Step-by-Step*: The first scan to trigger both is the LCM of 6 and 8. LCM(6, 8) = 24. (Result: **24th**)
- **Missing Digit X Digit**: Find the missing digit 'x' in the 5-digit number 74x52 such that the entire number is divisible by 9.
- *Step-by-Step*: For a number to be divisible by 9, the sum of its digits must be divisible by 9. Sum = 7 + 4 + x + 5 + 2 = 18 + x. Since 18 is divisible by 9, x can be 0 or 9. (Result: **9**)

### Practical Calculations

- **Financial Analyst Is Evaluating**: A financial analyst is evaluating an investment fund whose value grows by a factor represented by the expression (7^85 - 4^85). What is the units digit of this resulting value?
- *Step-by-Step*: Units digit of 7^85: 85 mod 4 = 1, so 7^1 = 7. Units digit of 4^85: 85 is odd, so 4^85 ends in 4. Subtracting them: 7 - 4 = 3. (Result: **3**)
- **Automated Warehouse Conveyor Belt**: An automated warehouse conveyor belt drops packages into three sorting bins every 35, 56, and 91 seconds respectively. If all three bins receive a package right now, after how many seconds will they next receive packages simultaneously?
- *Step-by-Step*: Find LCM of 35 (5*7), 56 (7*8), and 91 (7*13). LCM = 7 * 5 * 8 * 13 = 3640 seconds. (Result: **3640 seconds**)
- **Cryptographic Algorithm Processes Alphanumeric**: A cryptographic algorithm processes an alphanumeric key structured as 5928p62q. If this 8-digit number is completely divisible by 88, what is the numerical value of (p * q)?
- *Step-by-Step*: Since 88 = 8 * 11. Divisibility by 8 requires the last 3 digits (82q) to be divisible by 8, yielding q=4. Divisibility by 11 requires alternating sum difference to be a multiple of 11, yielding p=8. Thus, p * q = 8 * 4 = 32. (Result: **32**)
