# HCF of Fractions

In quant analysis and testing, **HCF of Fractions** is a core pattern area. Mastering these concepts and shortcut rules is essential for solving competitive exam puzzles and verbal tests efficiently.

## Core Concepts and Fundamental Patterns

The **Highest Common Factor (HCF)**, also known as Greatest Common Divisor (GCD), of two or more numbers is the greatest number that divides each of them exactly without leaving any remainder.

### Primary Rules

- **Base Property**: Initial variables map directly to their standard mathematical forms.
- **Transformation Rule**: Operations must follow order limits to prevent calculation errors.

### How to Calculate / Find - Step-by-Step Methodology

To solve problems relating to this topic, follow these structured guidelines:

- **Standard Variation 1: Direct HCF**: Finding the highest common factor. Prime factorization: $HCF(a, b) = \text{product of lowest powers of common primes}$.
- **Standard Variation 2: HCF with different remainders**: Find the greatest number dividing $A, B, C$ leaving remainders $x, y, z \Rightarrow$ Find $\text{HCF}(A-x, B-y, C-z)$.
- **Standard Variation 3: HCF with same remainder (unknown)**: Find the greatest number dividing $A, B, C$ leaving the same remainder in each case $\Rightarrow$ Find $\text{HCF}(|A-B|, |B-C|, |C-A|)$.
- **Standard Variation 4: Minimum rows/boxes packing**: When packing items of sizes $A, B, C$ in equal racks without mixing. First find HCF ($H$), then total racks required $= \frac{A}{H} + \frac{B}{H} + \frac{C}{H}$.
- **For Fractions**: $\text{HCF of Fractions} = \frac{\text{HCF of Numerators}}{\text{LCM of Denominators}}$.

### Practical Calculations

- **Artisan Is Cutting Three**: An artisan is cutting three metal ribbons of lengths 2/3 m, 4/9 m, and 5/6 m into equal maximum-sized smaller pieces without any waste. What is the length of each such piece?
- *Step-by-Step*: The maximum length of each piece is given by the HCF of fractions (2/3, 4/9, 5/6). HCF = HCF(2, 4, 5) / LCM(3, 9, 6) = 1 / 18. (Result: **1/18 m**)
- **Baker Has Three Batches**: A baker has three batches of flour mixtures weighing 3/4 kg, 9/8 kg, and 15/16 kg. She wants to divide them into packets of equal maximum weight. What is the weight of one such packet?
- *Step-by-Step*: HCF of fractions (3/4, 9/8, 15/16) = HCF(3, 9, 15) / LCM(4, 8, 16) = 3 / 16 kg. (Result: **3/16 kg**)

### Practical Calculations

- **Three Athletes Are Running**: Three athletes are running laps on a circular track, completing a lap in 4/5 minutes, 8/15 minutes, and 2/3 minutes respectively. If we need to find a common fractional standard time unit that divides each of their lap times completely, what is that maximum fraction?
- *Step-by-Step*: HCF of fractions (4/5, 8/15, 2/3) = HCF(4, 8, 2) / LCM(5, 15, 3) = 2 / 15. (Result: **2/15 min**)
- **Hardware Store Manager Packages**: A hardware store manager packages three types of copper wires measuring 5/2 meters, 15/4 meters, and 25/8 meters into equal maximum uniform lengths. What is the length of each uniform wire?
- *Step-by-Step*: HCF of fractions (5/2, 15/4, 25/8) = HCF(5, 15, 25) / LCM(2, 4, 8) = 5 / 8 m. (Result: **5/8 m**)
