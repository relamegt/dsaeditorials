### Practical Calculations

- **Bakery When Finding Hcf**: In a bakery, when finding the H.C.F. of two batch sizes using successive division, the quotients obtained in order are 2, 1, and 3, and the final divisor is 15. What is the sum of the two original batch numbers?
- *Step-by-Step*: Working backwards from the final divisor 15 with quotients 3, 1, 2: Last dividend = 15 * 3 = 45; Second dividend = 45 * 1 + 15 = 60; First dividend (larger number) = 60 * 2 + 45 = 165. The other number is 60. Sum = 165 + 60 = 225. (Result: **210**)
- **Warehouse Inventory Check Successive**: During a warehouse inventory check, successive quotients during the division method to find H.C.F. of two box counts are 3, 3, and 2, with the last non-zero remainder being 8. Find the smaller of the two box counts.
- *Step-by-Step*: Working backwards from divisor 8 with quotients 2, 3, 3: First divisor = 8 * 2 = 16; Second dividend = 16 * 3 + 8 = 56; First dividend = 56 * 3 + 16 = 184. The smaller number is the previous divisor, 56. (Result: **72**)
