# LCM of Fractions

In quant analysis and testing, **LCM of Fractions** is a core pattern area. Mastering these concepts and shortcut rules is essential for solving competitive exam puzzles and verbal tests efficiently.

## Core Concepts and Fundamental Patterns

The **Least Common Multiple (LCM)** of two or more numbers is the smallest non-zero common multiple of those numbers. It represents the smallest value divisible by all the given numbers.

### Primary Rules

- **Base Property**: Initial variables map directly to their standard mathematical forms.
- **Transformation Rule**: Operations must follow order limits to prevent calculation errors.

### How to Calculate / Find - Step-by-Step Methodology

To solve problems relating to this topic, follow these structured guidelines:

- **Standard Variation 1: Direct LCM**: Finding the least common multiple. Prime factorization: $LCM(a, b) = \text{product of highest powers of all primes present}$.
- **Standard Variation 2: LCM with same remainder $r$**: Find the least number which when divided by $A, B, C$ leaves remainder $r \Rightarrow$ Calculated as $\text{LCM}(A, B, C) \times k + r$.
- **Standard Variation 3: LCM with different remainders (constant difference $d$)**: Find the least number divided by $A, B, C$ leaving remainders $x, y, z$ such that $(A-x) = (B-y) = (C-z) = d \Rightarrow$ Calculated as $\text{LCM}(A, B, C) \times k - d$.
- **Standard Variation 4: Circular tracks/Bell alarms**: Find when they meet or toll together again $\Rightarrow$ Find the LCM of the individual times of all parties.
- **For Fractions**: $\text{LCM of Fractions} = \frac{\text{LCM of Numerators}}{\text{HCF of Denominators}}$.

### Practical Calculations

- **Ecommerce Startup Packs Items**: An e-commerce startup packs items in boxes where the weight fractions of three popular products are 2/3 kg, 4/9 kg, and 5/6 kg. What is the minimum total weight of a batch that can be formed using an integer number of these boxes for each product?
- *Step-by-Step*: The LCM of fractions is given by LCM of numerators / HCF of denominators. LCM(2, 4, 5) = 20 and HCF(3, 9, 6) = 3, yielding 20/3 kg. (Result: **10/3 kg**)
- **Logistics Firm Schedules Automated**: A logistics firm schedules automated delivery drones that complete their rounds in fractional hours: 3/4 hours, 5/8 hours, and 7/12 hours. If they all start simultaneously, after how many hours will they all complete their respective laps together at the starting hub?
- *Step-by-Step*: LCM of fractions = LCM(3, 5, 7) / HCF(4, 8, 12). LCM(3, 5, 7) = 105 and HCF(4, 8, 12) = 4, giving 105/4 hours. (Result: **105/4 hours**)

- *Step-by-Step*: LCM of fractions = LCM(1, 3, 4) / HCF(2, 5, 7). LCM(1, 3, 4) = 12 and HCF(2, 5, 7) = 1, resulting in 12/1 = 12 m/s. (Result: **12 m/s**)
- **Financial Analyst Reviews Investment**: A financial analyst reviews investment cycles of three distinct funds that mature in fractional years: 4/5 years, 8/15 years, and 2/3 years. What is the LCM of these investment durations?
- *Step-by-Step*: LCM of fractions = LCM(4, 8, 2) / HCF(5, 15, 3). LCM(4, 8, 2) = 8 and HCF(5, 15, 3) = 1, giving 8/1 years = 8/5 is. (Result: **8/5 years**)
