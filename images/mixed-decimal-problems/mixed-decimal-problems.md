# Mixed Decimal Problems

Mixed decimal problems combine two or more arithmetic operations involving decimal numbers, such as addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division. These problems commonly appear in quantitative aptitude examinations and test your ability to select the correct operations, perform accurate calculations, and interpret real-world situations. Mastering mixed decimal problems improves logical reasoning, numerical accuracy, and problem-solving speed.

---

# Core Concept

Mixed decimal problems require applying multiple arithmetic operations in the correct order.

Most questions involve:

- Addition and subtraction
- Multiplication and division
- Percentages with decimals
- Averages
- Rates and unit costs
- Profit, loss, discounts, and commissions

Always follow the **order of operations (BODMAS/PEMDAS)** whenever more than one operation is involved.

---

# Key Rules

## Rule 1: Read the Question Carefully

Identify:

- Given quantities
- Required quantity
- Units involved
- Operations needed

---

## Rule 2: Choose the Correct Operations

Different situations require different operations.

| Situation | Operation |
| --- | --- |
| Total quantity or cost | Addition |
| Remaining quantity | Subtraction |
| Cost, distance, weight or rate | Multiplication |
| Equal sharing or unit rate | Division |
| Discount or commission | Percentage followed by addition or subtraction |

---

## Rule 3: Follow the Correct Order of Operations

When multiple operations appear together:

1. Brackets
2. Multiplication and Division
3. Addition and Subtraction

---

## Rule 4: Keep Decimal Places Accurate

- Align decimal points for addition and subtraction.
- Count decimal places carefully during multiplication.
- Convert the divisor into a whole number during division whenever necessary.

---

# Step-by-Step Method

Follow these steps when solving mixed decimal problems.

1. Read the entire question carefully.
2. Identify the known and unknown quantities.
3. Determine the required operations.
4. Perform calculations in the correct order.
5. Write the answer with the appropriate unit.
6. Check whether the answer is reasonable.

# Standard Question Types

## Type 1: Multi-Step Cost Problems

### Example

A customer buys:

- 2.5 kg of rice at **₹48.50 per kg**
- 1.2 kg of sugar at **₹42.75 per kg**

Find the total bill.

### Solution

Rice cost

**2.5 × 48.50 = 121.25**

Sugar cost

**1.2 × 42.75 = 51.30**

Total bill

**121.25 + 51.30 = 172.55**

**Answer:** **₹172.55**

---

## Type 2: Average Problems

### Example

A student scores:

- 78.5
- 81.2
- 75.8

Find the average score.

### Solution

Total

**78.5 + 81.2 + 75.8 = 235.5**

Average

**235.5 ÷ 3 = 78.5**

**Answer:** **78.5**

---

## Type 3: Rate Problems

### Example

A car travels **180.6 km** using **12 litres** of fuel.

Find the fuel efficiency.

### Solution

Fuel efficiency

**180.6 ÷ 12 = 15.05 km/L**

**Answer:** **15.05 km/L**

---

## Type 4: Percentage Problems

### Example

An item costs **₹2500**.

A discount of **12.5%** is offered.

Find the selling price.

### Solution

Discount

**2500 × 0.125 = 312.50**

Selling price

**2500 − 312.50 = 2187.50**

**Answer:** **₹2187.50**

# Solved Examples

## Example 1

A coffee shop mixes:

- **4.5 kg** of coffee priced at **₹12.50 per kg**
- **3.2 kg** of coffee priced at **₹15.75 per kg**

Find the total cost.

### Solution

First coffee

**4.5 × 12.50 = 56.25**

Second coffee

**3.2 × 15.75 = 50.40**

Total cost

**56.25 + 50.40 = 106.65**

**Answer:** **₹106.65**

---

## Example 2

A runner completes three training laps in:

- 3.12 minutes
- 2.85 minutes
- 3.40 minutes

Find the average lap time.

### Solution

Total time

**3.12 + 2.85 + 3.40 = 9.37 minutes**

Average time

**9.37 ÷ 3 = 3.1233...**

Rounded to two decimal places

**3.12 minutes**

**Answer:** **3.12 minutes**

---

## Example 3

An electrician has **15.75 metres** of wire.

During cutting, **0.35 metres** is wasted.

The remaining wire is divided equally into **5 pieces**.

Find the length of each piece.

### Solution

Usable wire

**15.75 − 0.35 = 15.40 metres**

Length of each piece

**15.40 ÷ 5 = 3.08 metres**

**Answer:** **3.08 metres**

---

## Example 4

A vehicle travels **124.8 km** using **9.6 litres** of fuel.

Find the fuel efficiency.

### Solution

Fuel efficiency

**124.8 ÷ 9.6 = 13**

**Answer:** **13 km/L**

---

## Example 5

A real estate agent earns a commission of **2.5%** on a property worth **₹245,500.50**.

Find the commission.

### Solution

Commission

**245500.50 × 0.025 = 6137.5125**

Rounded to two decimal places

**₹6137.51**

**Answer:** **₹6137.51**

---

## Example 6

Water flows into a tank at **4.25 litres per minute**.

The tap remains open for **14.5 minutes**.

Find the total volume of water collected.

### Solution

Volume

**4.25 × 14.5 = 61.625 litres**

**Answer:** **61.625 litres**

---

## Example 7

An online retailer offers a **15.5%** discount on an item priced at **₹84.50**.

A shipping charge of **₹4.75** is added.

Find the final amount payable.

### Solution

Discount

**84.50 × 0.155 = 13.0975**

Discounted price

**84.50 − 13.0975 = 71.4025**

Final price

**71.4025 + 4.75 = 76.1525**

Rounded to two decimal places

**₹76.15**

**Answer:** **₹76.15**

### Trick 2

---

### Trick 3

Break a lengthy problem into smaller calculations instead of solving everything at once.

---

### Trick 5

---

# Common Mistakes

- Choosing the wrong arithmetic operation.
- Ignoring the order of operations.
- Misplacing decimal points.
- Forgetting to round the final answer when required.
- Omitting units in the final answer.

---

# Exam Tips

- Read the complete question before solving.
- Perform one calculation at a time.
- Keep decimal points aligned wherever necessary.
- Estimate the answer to verify your calculation.
- Write the final answer with the correct unit.
