# Division of Decimals

Division of decimals is a fundamental arithmetic operation frequently tested in quantitative aptitude examinations. It is widely used in problems involving money, measurements, speed, fuel consumption, unit rates, averages, and data interpretation. Mastering decimal division helps solve quantitative problems quickly and accurately.

---

# Core Concept

Dividing decimals follows the same process as dividing whole numbers. The key idea is to make the **divisor a whole number** before performing the division.

### Basic Rule

1. Count the decimal places in the divisor.
2. Move the decimal point in both the dividend and divisor the same number of places to the right.
3. Divide the resulting whole numbers.
4. Write the answer.

### Example

Find:

**12.5 ÷ 0.5**

Move the decimal point one place to the right in both numbers.

**125 ÷ 5 = 25**

**Answer:** **25**

---

# Key Rules

## Rule 1: Make the Divisor a Whole Number

Always remove the decimal from the divisor by multiplying both numbers by the same power of 10.

### Example

Find:

**9.6 ÷ 0.8**

Multiply both numbers by 10.

**96 ÷ 8 = 12**

**Answer:** **12**

---

## Rule 2: Division by 10, 100 and 1000

Move the decimal point to the left.

| Expression | Result |
| --- | --- |
| 45.6 ÷ 10 | 4.56 |
| 45.6 ÷ 100 | 0.456 |
| 45.6 ÷ 1000 | 0.0456 |

---

## Rule 3: Division by 0.1, 0.01 and 0.001

Dividing by numbers smaller than 1 increases the value.

| Expression | Result |
| --- | --- |
| 25 ÷ 0.1 | 250 |
| 25 ÷ 0.01 | 2500 |
| 25 ÷ 0.001 | 25000 |

---

## Rule 4: Simplify Before Dividing

Whenever possible, remove common factors after converting the divisor into a whole number.

### Example

Find:

**48.75 ÷ 3.25**

Multiply both numbers by 100.

**4875 ÷ 325**

Simplify.

**975 ÷ 65 = 195 ÷ 13 = 15**

**Answer:** **15**

---

# Step-by-Step Method

Follow these steps to divide decimal numbers.

1. Identify the divisor.
2. Convert the divisor into a whole number by moving the decimal point to the right.
3. Move the decimal point in the dividend by the same number of places.
4. Perform the division.
5. Simplify the answer if required.

# Standard Question Types

## Type 1: Decimal ÷ Whole Number

### Example

Find:

**7.5 ÷ 3**

### Solution

Divide directly.

**7.5 ÷ 3 = 2.5**

**Answer:** **2.5**

---

## Type 2: Decimal ÷ Decimal

### Example

Find:

**15.6 ÷ 1.2**

### Solution

Multiply both numbers by 10.

**156 ÷ 12 = 13**

**Answer:** **13**

---

## Type 3: Division by Powers of 10

### Example

Find:

**48.25 ÷ 100**

### Solution

Move the decimal point two places to the left.

**48.25 → 0.4825**

**Answer:** **0.4825**

---

## Type 4: Word Problems

Use division when the question asks:

- How many groups?
- Cost per item
- Speed
- Unit price
- Number of packets
- Number of pieces
- Number of batches
- Average quantity

# Solved Examples

## Example 1

A baker has **12.5 kg** of flour.

Each batch requires **0.5 kg**.

How many batches can be prepared?

### Solution

Move the decimal point one place.

**125 ÷ 5 = 25**

**Answer:** **25 batches**

---

## Example 2

A commuter travels **14.4 km**.

The scooter covers **1.2 km per unit of battery consumption**.

How many units of battery are consumed?

### Solution

Multiply both numbers by 10.

**144 ÷ 12 = 12**

**Answer:** **12 units**

---

## Example 3

How many strips can be cut?

# Competitive Exam Tricks

### Trick 1

Always remove the decimal point from the divisor first.

---

### Trick 2

Move the decimal point in both numbers by the same number of places.

---

### Trick 3

Estimate the answer before performing the calculation.

Example:

**49 ÷ 5 ≈ 10**

If your calculated answer is far from 10, recheck your work.

---

### Trick 4

Remember these useful facts:

- Dividing by **0.5** is the same as multiplying by **2**.
- Dividing by **0.25** is the same as multiplying by **4**.
- Dividing by **0.2** is the same as multiplying by **5**.
- Dividing by **0.1** is the same as multiplying by **10**.

These shortcuts save valuable time in competitive examinations.

---

# Common Mistakes

- Forgetting to move the decimal point in both the dividend and divisor.
- Moving the decimal point by different numbers of places.
- Performing incorrect whole-number division.
- Misplacing the decimal point in the quotient.
- Forgetting to simplify when possible.

---

# Exam Tips

- Always convert the divisor into a whole number before dividing.
- Estimate the answer to detect calculation errors.
- Memorize shortcuts for division by 0.5, 0.25, and 0.2.
- Practice both numerical calculations and word problems.
- Check the final answer by multiplying the quotient by the divisor.

---

# Summary

Division of decimals is performed by first converting the divisor into a whole number and then dividing as with whole numbers. By mastering decimal shifting, powers of 10, estimation techniques, and common shortcut values, you can solve decimal division problems quickly and accurately in competitive examinations.
