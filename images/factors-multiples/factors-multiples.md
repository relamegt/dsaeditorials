### Practical Calculations

- **Local Bakery Packs Cupcakes**: A local bakery packs cupcakes in boxes of 12. If they baked 156 cupcakes today, how many full boxes were packed without any leftover cupcakes?
- *Step-by-Step*: Divide the total number of cupcakes by the number of cupcakes per box: 156 / 12 = 13. Thus, exactly 13 boxes are packed. (Result: **13**)
- **Office Security Guard Has**: An office security guard has three rounds of patrols lasting every 4, 6, and 8 minutes respectively. If he starts all three simultaneously at 9:00 AM, after how many minutes will all three alarms ring together again?
- *Step-by-Step*: The time when all patrols coincide again is given by the least common multiple (LCM) of 4, 6, and 8, which is 24. (Result: **24**)
- **Tech Firm Distributes Merchandise**: A tech firm distributes merchandise kits consisting of branded hoodies and mugs. If they have 72 hoodies and 90 mugs, what is the maximum number of identical kits they can make such that no items are left over?
- *Step-by-Step*: The maximum number of identical kits is the greatest common divisor (GCD) of 72 and 90, which is 18. (Result: **18**)
- **Which Of Following Numbers**: Which of the following numbers is a multiple of 17?
- *Step-by-Step*: Dividing 238 by 17 gives 14 with a remainder of 0. Thus, 238 is a multiple of 17 (17 x 14 = 238). (Result: **238**)

### Practical Calculations

- **Logistics Company Wants Arrange**: A logistics company wants to arrange 330 container boxes in rows such that each row contains the exact same number of boxes. If each row must have more than 10 but fewer than 30 boxes, how many valid row sizes are possible?
- *Step-by-Step*: We need to find the factors of 330 that lie strictly between 10 and 30. The prime factorization of 330 is 2 x 3 x 5 x 11. The factors between 10 and 30 are 11, 15, and 22, giving 3 valid sizes. (Result: **3**)
- **Sum Of All Positive**: Find the sum of all positive distinct factors of the number 180.
- *Step-by-Step*: The prime factorization of 180 is 2^2 x 3^2 x 5^1. The sum of factors is (1+2+4)(1+3+9)(1+5) = 7 x 13 x 6 = 546. (Result: **546**)
- **Three Runner Athletes Run**: Three runner athletes run around a circular track of circumference 1200 meters at speeds of 50 m/min, 60 m/min, and 75 m/min respectively. After how many minutes will they meet at the starting point for the first time?
- *Step-by-Step*: Time taken by each to complete one round: 1200/50 = 24 min, 1200/60 = 20 min, 1200/75 = 16 min. The LCM of 24, 20, and 16 is 240 minutes. (Result: **120 minutes**)

### Practical Calculations

- **Online Retail Warehouse Has**: An online retail warehouse has a stock of 420 red shirts and 588 blue shirts. They want to pack them into identical gift boxes such that every box contains the same number of red shirts and the same number of blue shirts. What is the maximum number of boxes that can be packed?
- *Step-by-Step*: The maximum number of identical boxes is the HCF of 420 and 588. Prime factorization: 420 = 2^2 x 3 x 5 x 7, 588 = 2^2 x 3 x 7^2. HCF = 2^2 x 3 x 7 = 84. (Result: **84**)
- **How Many Factors Of**: How many factors of the number 1080 are perfect squares?
- *Step-by-Step*: Prime factorization of 1080 is 2^3 x 3^3 x 5^1. Any factor of 1080 is of the form 2^a x 3^b x 5^c where 0  \lt = a  \lt = 3, 0  \lt = b  \lt = 3, 0  \lt = c  \lt = 1. For the factor to be a perfect square, a, b, and c must be even integers. Thus, a can be 0 or 2 (2 choices), b can be 0 or 2 (2 choices), and c must be 0 (1 choice). Total square factors = 2 x 2 x 1 = 4. (Result: **4**)
- **Let N X X**: Let N = 2^4 x 3^5 x 5^2. If P is the product of all distinct factors of N, how many trailing zeros does the number P have in its base-10 representation?
- *Step-by-Step*: Total number of factors of N is (4+1)(5+1)(2+1) = 5 x 6 x 3 = 90 factors. The product of all factors of N is N^(total factors / 2) = (2^4 x 3^5 x 5^2)^45. The number of trailing zeros is determined by the power of 5 in the prime factorization of P, which is 2 x 45 = 90. (Result: **90**)
