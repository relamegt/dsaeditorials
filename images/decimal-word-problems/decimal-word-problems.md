# Decimal Word Problems

Decimal word problems combine arithmetic operations with real-life situations. These questions frequently appear in quantitative aptitude examinations and test your ability to identify the correct mathematical operation before performing decimal calculations. Mastering decimal word problems improves logical thinking, calculation speed, and problem-solving accuracy.

---

# Core Concept

A decimal word problem requires you to convert a real-life situation into a mathematical expression involving decimal numbers.

The most important step is identifying **which arithmetic operation** the question requires.

| Situation | Operation |
| --- | --- |
| Total or combined quantity | Addition |
| Remaining amount or difference | Subtraction |
| Repeated quantity or total cost | Multiplication |
| Equal sharing or unit rate | Division |

---

# Key Rules

## Rule 1: Read the Question Carefully

Identify:

- Given quantities
- Required quantity
- Units
- Keywords indicating the required operation

---

## Rule 2: Choose the Correct Operation

Common keywords include:

| Keywords | Operation |
| --- | --- |
| Total, altogether, combined, sum | Addition |
| Left, remaining, difference, balance | Subtraction |
| Each, every, total cost, repeated | Multiplication |
| Per, each group, average, equally, rate | Division |

---

## Rule 3: Keep Units Consistent

Always ensure that all quantities are expressed in the same units before performing calculations.

Example:

- metres with metres
- kilograms with kilograms
- litres with litres
- rupees with rupees

---

## Rule 4: Estimate the Answer

Estimate mentally before calculating.

Example:

₹19.85 + ₹10.20 ≈ ₹30

If your calculated answer is very different, check your work.

---

# Step-by-Step Method

Follow these steps to solve decimal word problems.

1. Read the question carefully.
2. Identify the given information.
3. Determine what needs to be found.
4. Choose the correct mathematical operation.
5. Perform the decimal calculation.
6. Write the answer with the correct unit.
7. Verify whether the answer is reasonable.

---

# Standard Question Types

## Type 1: Addition Problems

### Example

A shopkeeper sells items costing **₹12.45**, **₹8.60**, and **₹5.25**.

Find the total bill.

### Solution

Total cost

**12.45 + 8.60 + 5.25 = 26.30**

**Answer:** **₹26.30**

---

## Type 2: Subtraction Problems

### Example

A water tank contains **35.80 litres** of water.

After using **12.45 litres**, how much water remains?

### Solution

Remaining water

**35.80 − 12.45 = 23.35**

**Answer:** **23.35 litres**

---

## Type 3: Multiplication Problems

### Example

A notebook costs **₹18.75**.

Find the cost of **8 notebooks**.

### Solution

Total cost

**18.75 × 8 = 150.00**

**Answer:** **₹150**

---

## Type 4: Division Problems

### Example

A ribbon measuring **15.6 metres** is cut into pieces of **1.2 metres** each.

How many pieces are obtained?

### Solution

Number of pieces

**15.6 ÷ 1.2 = 13**

**Answer:** **13 pieces**

# Solved Examples

## Example 1

A baker uses **1.75 kg** of flour for cookies and **2.25 kg** for bread.

If she purchases **10 kg** of flour, how much flour remains?

### Solution

Total flour used

**1.75 + 2.25 = 4.00 kg**

Remaining flour

**10.00 − 4.00 = 6.00 kg**

**Answer:** **6 kg**

---

## Example 2

An office worker travels **4.25 km** to work and the same distance back home each day.

How far does the worker travel in **5 days**?

### Solution

Daily distance

**4.25 × 2 = 8.50 km**

Weekly distance

**8.50 × 5 = 42.50 km**

**Answer:** **42.5 km**

---

## Example 3

Rohan buys:

- Apples for **₹3.40**
- Milk for **₹2.85**
- Cereal for **₹4.15**

If he pays **₹20**, how much change does he receive?

### Solution

Total bill

**3.40 + 2.85 + 4.15 = 10.40**

Change

**20.00 − 10.40 = 9.60**

**Answer:** **₹9.60**

---

## Example 4

A runner completes three practice runs in:

- 3.60 minutes
- 3.45 minutes
- 3.90 minutes

Find the average running time.

### Solution

Total time

**3.60 + 3.45 + 3.90 = 10.95 minutes**

Average

**10.95 ÷ 3 = 3.65 minutes**

**Answer:** **3.65 minutes**

---

## Example 5

A courier company charges a fixed fee of **₹5.00** plus **₹1.25 per kilogram**.

A parcel weighs **6.4 kg**.

Find the shipping cost.

### Solution

Weight charge

**6.4 × 1.25 = 8.00**

Total cost

**5.00 + 8.00 = 13.00**

**Answer:** **₹13.00**

---

## Example 6

A water tank contains **45.6 litres** of water.

Water is drained at **3.8 litres per hour**.

How many hours will it take to empty the tank?

### Solution

Time

**45.6 ÷ 3.8 = 12**

**Answer:** **12 hours**

---

## Example 7

A carpenter has a timber plank measuring **7.5 metres**.

He cuts **3 pieces**, each measuring **1.85 metres**.

Find the remaining length.

### Solution

Length removed

**3 × 1.85 = 5.55 metres**

Remaining length

**7.50 − 5.55 = 1.95 metres**

**Answer:** **1.95 metres**

# Competitive Exam Tricks

### Trick 1

Read the last sentence first to identify what the question is asking.

---

### Trick 2

Circle important numbers before performing calculations.

---

### Trick 3

Watch for operation keywords.

- Total → Add
- Left → Subtract
- Each → Multiply
- Per → Divide

---

### Trick 4

Estimate the answer before calculating.

This helps detect arithmetic mistakes quickly.

---

### Trick 5

Always write units with the final answer.

---

# Common Mistakes

- Choosing the wrong mathematical operation.
- Ignoring units.
- Misplacing the decimal point.
- Forgetting to calculate the final quantity after finding an intermediate result.
- Rounding too early in the calculation.

---

# Exam Tips

- Read the entire question before solving.
- Underline important numbers and keywords.
- Perform calculations carefully with decimal points aligned.
- Check whether the final answer has the correct unit.
- Estimate the result to verify your calculation.

---

# Summary

Decimal word problems require translating real-life situations into mathematical expressions involving decimal numbers. The key to solving these questions is identifying the correct operation, performing accurate decimal calculations, and expressing the final answer with the appropriate unit. Regular practice with addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division word problems builds confidence and improves performance in competitive examinations.
