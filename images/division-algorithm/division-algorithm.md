# Division Algorithm

In quant analysis and testing, **Division Algorithm** is a core pattern area. Mastering these concepts and shortcut rules is essential for solving competitive exam puzzles and verbal tests efficiently.

## Core Concepts and Fundamental Patterns

The foundation of **Division Algorithm** relies on structured rules and operations. Understanding the base properties allows for rapid simplification of complex inputs.

### Primary Rules

- **Base Property**: Initial variables map directly to their standard mathematical forms.
- **Transformation Rule**: Operations must follow order limits to prevent calculation errors.

### Practical Calculations

- **When Consignment Of Organic**: When a consignment of organic apples is packed in boxes of 12, exactly 5 apples are left over. If the same consignment is packed in boxes of 15, how many apples will be left over?
- *Step-by-Step*: Let N be the total number of apples. N = 12q + 5. Testing values of q starting from 1 gives N = 17, 29, 41, 53, 65, etc. For N = 65, 65 divided by 15 gives a quotient of 4 and a remainder of 5. (Result: **5**)
- **Bakery Software Module Inventory**: In a bakery software module, an inventory batch number divided by 13 leaves a remainder of 7. What will be the remainder when twice this inventory batch number is divided by 13?
- *Step-by-Step*: If number N = 13q + 7, then 2N = 2(13q + 7) = 26q + 14 = 26q + 13 + 1 = 13(2q + 1) + 1. Thus, the remainder is 1. (Result: **3**)
- **Textile Mill Manager Divides**: A textile mill manager divides a roll of fabric into pieces of 8 meters, leaving a 3-meter remnant. What is the remainder if the total length of the fabric roll is divided by 4?
- *Step-by-Step*: Let the length be L = 8q + 3. Since 8q is divisible by 4, the remainder depends only on 3 divided by 4, which leaves 3. (Result: **3**)
- **Event Coordinator Distributes Promotional**: An event coordinator distributes 150 promotional kits among attendees. Each attendee receives 7 kits, and a few kits remain unassigned. How many kits remain unassigned?
- *Step-by-Step*: Dividing 150 by 7 gives 150 = 7 * 21 + 3. (Result: **3**)

### Practical Calculations

- **Logistics Audit Shipment Identifier**: During a logistics audit, a shipment identifier N leaves a remainder of 9 when divided by 16. What remainder does N leave when divided by 8?
- *Step-by-Step*: N = 16q + 9 = 16q + 8 + 1 = 8(2q + 1) + 1. When divided by 8, the remainder is 1. (Result: **1**)
- **Financial Auditor Checks Ledger**: A financial auditor checks a ledger where an amount N yields a remainder of 4 when divided by 5, and 1 when divided by 2. What is the remainder when N is divided by 10?
- *Step-by-Step*: Testing numbers of the form 5q + 4: 4 (rem 0 for 2), 9 (rem 1 for 2). Thus N = 9, 19, 29, etc. All these numbers leave a remainder of 9 when divided by 10. (Result: **9**)
- **Hardware Warehouse Nails Are**: In a hardware warehouse, nails are packed into boxes of 11, leaving a remainder of 4. If 5 times the total number of nails is packed into boxes of 11, what is the remainder?
- *Step-by-Step*: If N = 11q + 4, then 5N = 5(11q + 4) = 55q + 20 = 55q + 11 + 9 = 11(5q + 1) + 9. The remainder is 9. (Result: **9**)
