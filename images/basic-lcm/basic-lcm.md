# Basic LCM

In quant analysis and testing, **Basic LCM** is a core pattern area. Mastering these concepts and shortcut rules is essential for solving competitive exam puzzles and verbal tests efficiently.

## Core Concepts and Fundamental Patterns

The **Least Common Multiple (LCM)** of two or more numbers is the smallest non-zero common multiple of those numbers. It represents the smallest value divisible by all the given numbers.

### Primary Rules

- **Base Property**: Initial variables map directly to their standard mathematical forms.
- **Transformation Rule**: Operations must follow order limits to prevent calculation errors.

### How to Calculate / Find - Step-by-Step Methodology

To solve problems relating to this topic, follow these structured guidelines:

- **Standard Variation 1: Direct LCM**: Finding the least common multiple. Prime factorization: $LCM(a, b) = \text{product of highest powers of all primes present}$.
- **Standard Variation 2: LCM with same remainder $r$**: Find the least number which when divided by $A, B, C$ leaves remainder $r \Rightarrow$ Calculated as $\text{LCM}(A, B, C) \times k + r$.
- **Standard Variation 3: LCM with different remainders (constant difference $d$)**: Find the least number divided by $A, B, C$ leaving remainders $x, y, z$ such that $(A-x) = (B-y) = (C-z) = d \Rightarrow$ Calculated as $\text{LCM}(A, B, C) \times k - d$.
- **Standard Variation 4: Circular tracks/Bell alarms**: Find when they meet or toll together again $\Rightarrow$ Find the LCM of the individual times of all parties.
- **For Fractions**: $\text{LCM of Fractions} = \frac{\text{LCM of Numerators}}{\text{HCF of Denominators}}$.

### Practical Calculations

- **Two Security Alarms Warehouse**: Two security alarms at a warehouse ring at intervals of 4 minutes and 6 minutes respectively. If they ring together right now, after how many minutes will they ring together next?
- *Step-by-Step*: The time they ring together next is the Least Common Multiple (LCM) of 4 and 6, which is 12 minutes. (Result: **12 minutes**)
- **Courier Delivery Drone Is**: A courier delivery drone is programmed to return to its docking station every 5 hours, while another drone returns every 8 hours. If both start their maintenance cycle simultaneously, after how many hours will they be at the docking station at the same time?
- *Step-by-Step*: The moment they meet again at the dock is given by the LCM of 5 and 8, which is 40 hours. (Result: **40 hours**)

## Advanced Patterns and Complex Forms

For higher difficulty levels, variables are nested, requiring secondary substitutions and multi-step mathematical reasoning.

### Analytical Rules

- **Substitution Method**: Complex terms are renamed temporarily to make the main logic visible.
- **Double Expansion**: Factoring or simplifying recursively until the simplest form is reached.

### Practical Calculations

- **Digital Clock Display Green**: In a digital clock display, a green light flashes every 9 seconds and a blue light flashes every 12 seconds. If they flash together at the 0th second, after how many seconds will they flash together again?
- *Step-by-Step*: The simultaneous flashing occurs at intervals equal to the LCM of 9 and 12, which is 36 seconds. (Result: **36 seconds**)
- **Bakery Packages Cookies Boxes**: A bakery packages cookies in boxes of 10 and muffins in boxes of 15. What is the minimum total number of cookies the bakery must bake so that they can be packed completely into boxes of either size without any remainder?
- *Step-by-Step*: The minimum number of items required is the LCM of 10 and 15, which equals 30. (Result: **30**)

## Solving Complex Basic LCM Scenarios

Applying logical equations to word problems involves isolating unknowns and analyzing constraints.

### Higher-Level Rules

- **System Modeling**: Translate word puzzles into raw equations or logical trees.
- **Verification**: Test the boundary conditions of your output to ensure validity.

### Practical Calculations

- **Three Runners Practicing Circular**: Three runners practicing on a circular athletic track take 12 seconds, 16 seconds, and 24 seconds respectively to complete one full lap. If they start running from the same starting line at the same instant, after how many seconds will they all cross the starting line together again?
- *Step-by-Step*: The time when all runners meet at the start line is the LCM of 12, 16, and 24, which is 48 seconds. (Result: **48 seconds**)

## Practical Application Scenarios: Logical Problem Solving

- A financial auditing ledger registers a transaction query: *"An IT company distributes promotional keychains in packs of 12 and tech stickers in packs of 18. An event manager wants to distribute identical welcome kits with both items such that no items are left over. What is the least number of keychains the company must buy?"*

  The verification engine reconciles accounts to record: To have equal pairs, the total count must be a multiple of both 12 and 18, which is their LCM, 36.

- An e-commerce pricing engine adjusts stock discounts: *"Three automated production line sensors reset their diagnostic routines every 15 minutes, 20 minutes, and 30 minutes respectively. If all three reset simultaneously at 10:00 AM, at what time will they next reset together?"*

  The system updates the inventory database to determine: The LCM of 15, 20, and 30 is 60 minutes (1 hour). Adding 1 hour to 10:00 AM gives 11:00 AM.
